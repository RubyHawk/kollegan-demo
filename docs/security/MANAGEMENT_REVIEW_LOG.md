# Management Review Log

Owner: Management  
Review cadence: At least annually and after major ISMS changes  
Status: Empty register as of 2026-04-24

Use this register for completed management reviews only. Do not commit confidential financial data or sensitive customer material.

## Record Rules

- Add one row per completed management review.
- Record the key inputs reviewed, such as risks, incidents, audit findings, metrics, supplier issues, and corrective actions.
- Record decisions, owners, and due dates for follow-up actions.
- Link supporting notes, tickets, or meeting minutes when appropriate.
- Use `MANAGEMENT_REVIEW_AGENDA.md` to prepare the inputs and capture the required review topics.

## Entry Workflow

- Follow `OPERATIONAL_RECORD_ENTRY_STANDARD.md` before editing this log.
- Use `MANAGEMENT_REVIEW_AGENDA.md` to prepare the required inputs and decision topics.
- Add a row only after the management review is complete and decisions/actions are known.
- If this is the first committed row, change the status line to `Active register; last updated YYYY-MM-DD`.
- After committing the row, remove the matching `Open gap as of ...` row from `AUDIT_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md` and regenerate `READINESS_STATUS.md`, `OPERATIONAL_CLOSEOUT_STATUS.md`, and `PLAN_STATUS.md`.

## Entry Template

Use this row shape for the next completed record:

| YYYY-MM-DD | participant list | reviewed inputs | decisions summary | actions + owners | YYYY-MM-DD or N/A | meeting note / ticket |
