# Management Review Agenda

Owner: Management  
Review cadence: Before each management review  
Status: Baseline workflow

Use this agenda to run management reviews for the Kollegan ERP ISMS. Keep confidential financial details, customer escalations, and sensitive staffing notes outside the repo when necessary.

## Required Inputs

- Current `docs/security/RISK_REGISTER.md`
- Current `docs/security/STATEMENT_OF_APPLICABILITY.md`
- Latest `docs/security/AUDIT_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md`
- Latest entries from:
  - `ACCESS_REVIEW_LOG.md`
  - `RESTORE_TEST_LOG.md`
  - `INTERNAL_AUDIT_LOG.md`
  - `FEATURE_FLAG_ROLLOUT_LOG.md`
  - `INCIDENT_POSTMORTEM_LOG.md`
  - `VULNERABILITY_REVIEW_LOG.md`

## Agenda

1. Confirm scope, major system/vendor changes, and any pending scope decisions.
2. Review top risks, overdue actions, and treatment progress.
3. Review internal-audit findings and corrective-action status.
4. Review incidents, vulnerability trends, restore-test status, and access-review status.
5. Review release/change-management evidence for major architecture or production changes.
6. Confirm decisions, owners, and due dates.
7. Record the outcome in `MANAGEMENT_REVIEW_LOG.md`.

## Expected Outputs

- Decisions made.
- Follow-up actions with owners and due dates.
- Escalated risks or resource needs.
- Entry in `MANAGEMENT_REVIEW_LOG.md`.
