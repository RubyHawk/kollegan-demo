# Risk Methodology

Owner: ISMS Manager  
Review cadence: Quarterly  
Status: Draft baseline

## Scoring

Likelihood and impact are scored from 1 to 5.

Risk score:

```txt
likelihood × impact
```

Severity:

- 1-4: Low
- 5-9: Medium
- 10-16: High
- 17-25: Critical

## Required Fields

Each risk must include:

- ID,
- title,
- description,
- asset or process,
- likelihood,
- impact,
- inherent score,
- treatment decision,
- owner,
- target date,
- residual score,
- evidence links,
- next review date.

## Treatment Decisions

- Mitigate
- Accept
- Transfer
- Avoid

Accepted risks require owner approval and review date.
